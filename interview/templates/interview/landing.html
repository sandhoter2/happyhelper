Questions people ask first

Does HappyHelper join my call?

No. Nothing joins the call as a participant. HappyHelper runs in a browser tab or in the desktop overlay and listens through your microphone. The overlay can also pick up your computer's audio.

Which call apps does it work with?

Any of them. It listens to the conversation instead of plugging into the app, so Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and calls on speaker all work the same way.

Which languages can it follow?

You choose the language when you create a session: English (US, UK or India), Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Chinese, Korean or Portuguese.

Which AI models does it use?

Speech is transcribed with Whisper, running on Groq. Answers come from the model set for the server, through Groq or OpenRouter.

What happens to my audio and transcripts?

Audio from a session is uploaded to the HappyHelper server, stored with that session, and sent to the transcription provider to be turned into text. The transcript and the suggested answers are saved to your account so you can review and export them. Do not record anything you are not allowed to share.

Can other people on the call see it?

The desktop overlay is left out of screen shares and recordings. A browser tab is visible if you share that tab or your whole screen. Many employers do not allow outside help in interviews, and recording laws differ by place, so check the rules that apply to you.
